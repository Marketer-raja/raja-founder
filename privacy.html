Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Raja Founder (“we”, “us”) is a directory of founders around the world. This policy explains what information we collect when you use rajafounder.com and how we use it.

Information we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us:

  • Newsletter: your email address when you join the community.
  • Write to us / nominate a founder: your name, email address and message when you use the contact pop-up.
  • Founder profiles: public information about founders (name, company, role, country, industry, photo, story and links) that we publish in the directory.

How we use it

  • To send the newsletter you signed up for.
  • To reply to your messages and nominations.
  • To create, update and maintain founder profiles.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

We do not sell your personal information.

Storage and service providers

Submissions are stored with our database provider (Supabase). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.

Cookies and local storage

We may store a small item in your browser (for example, to remember that you have seen the pop-up). We do not use it to track you across other sites.

Founder profile requests

If you are listed in the directory and want your profile corrected or removed, use the Write to us form and tell us which profile it is.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you submitted, or unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time, by contacting us through the Write to us form.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the Write to us form.
