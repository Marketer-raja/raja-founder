About Raja Founder

Raja Founder is a growing directory of founders from around the world, with real stories, real journeys and real lessons from the people turning ideas into companies.

What we do

Every great company starts with one person's decision to begin. We bring those people together in one place, so that you can find who is building what, where they are building it, and how they got started.

  • Founder directory: search by name, company or country, or filter by industry, and open a full profile for each founder.
  • Founders by region: see where founders are building across Asia, Europe, North America, Africa, South America and Oceania.
  • Stories: read how founders started, what went wrong, and what worked.

Who it is for

  • Aspiring founders looking for examples and lessons from people who have already started.
  • Students and researchers exploring entrepreneurship across countries and industries.
  • Founders who want to be visible, connected and part of a wider community.

Our approach

Each profile shows the founder's company, role (founder or co-founder), country and industry, along with a short story. We aim to keep profiles accurate and respectful. If you spot a mistake or would like a profile changed or removed, please let us know and we will review it promptly.
